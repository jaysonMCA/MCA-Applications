# Output Template

Use this structure exactly. Do not add extra output sections.

```markdown
# [Company Name] | Executive DD

**As of:** [DD Month YYYY, timezone]
**News window:** [DD Month YYYY] to [DD Month YYYY]
**Official:** [Website] | [Primary profile if relevant]

## Bottom Line

[2 sentences. State what the company is, the most important commercial signal, and the central diligence tension.]

## Company Snapshot

| Field | Read |
|---|---|
| What it does | [Plain English] |
| Customer | [Buyer or user] |
| Business model | [How it makes or expects to make money] |
| Stage and traction | [Only sourced evidence] |
| Leadership and base | [Key leaders and geography] |
| Venture lens | [Compressed PRIME read, with unknowns stated] |

## Last 30 Days

1. **[DD Month YYYY] | [Development].** [What happened and why it matters.] [Source](URL)
2. **[DD Month YYYY] | [Development].** [What happened and why it matters.] [Source](URL)

[If none: No material company specific news was located from [start date] through [end date] across the reviewed official and independent sources.]

## DD Watchouts

1. **[High, Medium, or Low] | [Confirmed, Reported, Alleged, Unverified, or Not found] | [Issue].** [Fact, evidence gap, and why it matters.] [Source](URL)
2. **[High, Medium, or Low] | [Evidence status] | [Issue].** [Fact, evidence gap, and why it matters.] [Source](URL)

## Call Priorities

1. [Question that tests the biggest commercial or strategic assumption.]
2. [Question that resolves the most material risk or contradiction.]
3. [Question that tests traction, economics, structure, or execution.]

## Sources

[Compact list of 5 to 10 unique sources, with title, publisher, date, and URL.]
```

## Section Notes

Title: Use the resolved brand or legal name only. Do not include uncertainty in the title if identity is unresolved; stop and ask for clarification instead.

Metadata: Include the exact research date, timezone, exact 30 day window, official website, and primary official profile when relevant. Do not use relative dates.

Bottom Line: Write 2 sentences that identify the company, the strongest commercial signal, and the central diligence tension. Do not repeat the full snapshot.

Company Snapshot: Use sourced plain-English facts. Include unknowns where evidence is missing. Do not use marketing copy or unsupported superlatives.

Last 30 Days: Include 1 to 5 material company specific developments with absolute dates and URLs. If none are found, use the no-news sentence exactly with the searched dates. Do not include sector-only news, duplicates, or old events republished without a new development.

DD Watchouts: Include 1 to 5 material issues with severity, evidence status, and source URL. Include evidence gaps and why the issue matters. Do not present allegations as facts.

Call Priorities: Include exactly 3 questions. Each question should test a material commercial, strategic, risk, traction, economics, structural, or execution assumption.

Sources: Provide 5 to 10 unique source URLs with title, publisher, date, and URL. Keep this compact and do not repeat duplicate URLs.

## Style Rules

1. Direct and analytical.
2. Plain English.
3. No hype.
4. No generic introductions.
5. No unsupported superlatives.
6. No repeated facts across sections.
7. No more than 5 recent developments.
8. No more than 5 DD watchouts.
9. Exactly 3 call priorities.
10. Use digits for numbers.
11. Use absolute dates.
12. Keep facts and interpretation visibly separate.
13. Use links inline and retain the compact Sources section for auditability.
