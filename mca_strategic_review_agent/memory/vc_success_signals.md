# VC And PE Success Signals

Fundability and company quality are related but separate. At early stages, team quality can drive funding confidence. As a company matures, evidence from customer behavior, retention, distribution, economics, and repeatability should matter more.

Source base:

- `references/methodology/hash_vc_evaluation_playbook.md`
- `references/research/early_stage_vc_research.md`

Research window for the current evidence base: 2023 to 2026.

## Pre-Seed Priorities

Use these weights as the default internal scorecard:

| Dimension | Weight |
| --- | ---: |
| Team and founder-market fit | 22% |
| Problem severity and customer insight | 15% |
| Market size and timing | 15% |
| Product differentiation and moat | 12% |
| Distribution and GTM | 10% |
| Traction and retention | 7% |
| Unit economics and capital efficiency | 6% |
| Business model and pricing | 5% |
| Competition, regulatory, and geographic risk | 4% |
| Round, valuation, and milestone logic | 4% |

Do not automatically punish a pre-seed company for limited revenue, limited product, or limited retention data. Ask what credible evidence should exist for this stage and category.

Pre-seed means underwriting the hypothesis: people, insight, problem, timing, learning speed, distribution wedge, and raise logic.

## Seed Priorities

Use these weights as the default internal scorecard:

| Dimension | Weight |
| --- | ---: |
| Team and founder-market fit | 15% |
| Problem severity and customer insight | 10% |
| Market size and timing | 12% |
| Product differentiation and moat | 12% |
| Distribution and GTM | 12% |
| Traction and retention | 18% |
| Unit economics and capital efficiency | 10% |
| Business model and pricing | 5% |
| Competition, regulatory, and geographic risk | 3% |
| Round, valuation, and milestone logic | 3% |

At seed, founder narrative should not compensate for weak real-world evidence as much as it can at pre-seed.

Seed means underwriting emerging evidence: customer pull, repeat behavior, retention, distribution repeatability, revenue quality, economics, and the path to the next financeable milestone.

## Evidence Hierarchy

- Signed is stronger than verbal.
- Paying is stronger than pilot.
- Repeat behavior is stronger than signup.
- Cohort retention is stronger than aggregate growth.
- Net economics are stronger than headline revenue or GMV.
- Active advisor involvement is stronger than advisor logos.
- Specific distribution channels are stronger than generic GTM language.

## Internal Rating Scale

- 5: exceptional stage-appropriate evidence that materially reduces risk.
- 4: strong evidence with manageable unanswered questions.
- 3: plausible thesis but materially unproven.
- 2: weak evidence, meaningful concerns, or contradictory signals.
- 1: fundamental issue that may break the thesis.

## Hard Gates

Surface hard gates before numerical scoring:

- Integrity gate: fabricated traction, misrepresented partnerships, hidden material information, or evidence of dishonesty.
- Feasibility gate: core technical, regulatory, economic, or operational assumption appears unlikely to work.
- Commitment gate: founders lack sufficient commitment.
- Economics gate: the business structurally loses money without a credible path to better economics.
- Venture return gate: even a strong outcome cannot justify valuation and likely dilution.
