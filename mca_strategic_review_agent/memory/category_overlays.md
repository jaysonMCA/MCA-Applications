# Category Overlays

Use this file for category-specific diligence logic. Add only reusable rules, not company-specific analysis.

## Web3

- Separate protocol, marketplace, infrastructure, financial product, and services narratives.
- Test token necessity, custody exposure, regulatory exposure, liquidity assumptions, wallet friction, and ecosystem dependence.
- Treat advisor and partner logos as weak evidence unless activity is clear.
- Do not equate wallets, TVL, token price, transactions, or community size with sustainable adoption. Investigate real users, organic fees, incentive-adjusted activity, liquidity depth, treasury, token unlocks, security, and jurisdiction.

## AI

- Test model dependency, data advantage, workflow ownership, switching costs, model costs, defensibility against incumbents, and quality evaluation.
- Separate demo quality from retained usage.
- Treat fast ARR and strong demos as positive but incomplete signals until retention, gross margin after inference, reliability, and workflow ownership are visible.

## SaaS

- Test buyer clarity, pain frequency, implementation burden, retention, expansion path, sales motion, and payback.
- Distinguish pilots from production usage.

## Marketplace

- Test supply acquisition, demand concentration, trust, liquidity, take rate, disintermediation, fraud, and repeat behavior.
- Identify whether the company is solving cold start, quality control, or distribution.
- For physical non-fungible goods, test condition variance, authentication, custody, shipping, insurance, expected failed matches, who pays when a transaction breaks, and what keeps large matched trades on-platform.

## Consumer

- Test frequency, retention, distribution, monetization timing, brand, community, and channel dependence.
- Treat waitlists and social attention as preliminary evidence unless repeated usage exists.

## Fintech

- Test regulatory perimeter, licensing, compliance costs, partner-bank or platform dependence, fraud risk, trust, and unit economics.
