# Diligence Question Bank

Use this as a reusable source for reviewer interviews, research planning, and final next-step questions. Select only the questions that gate conviction for the specific company.

## Team

- Why are these founders unusually positioned to solve this problem?
- Are all core founders full-time? If not, what specifically triggers full-time commitment and when?
- Are there split-focus risks, related projects, IP conflicts, or other obligations?
- Which advisors are contractual and active, and what have they concretely done so far?

## Market And Timing

- What changed in the last 2 to 3 years that makes this possible now?
- What is the realistic serviceable market, not just the headline TAM?
- What is the initial segment most likely to adopt first?
- What expansion path makes this venture-scale if the wedge works?

## Problem And Wedge

- Is the problem urgent, frequent, expensive, risk-creating, or already consuming resources?
- What workaround do customers use today?
- Is this a painkiller, a hard fact customers have learned to tolerate, or a future-vision behavior change?
- What is useful before the network, data set, or marketplace is complete?

## Product And Moat

- What does the company understand that competitors do not?
- What gets stronger as the company succeeds?
- If the stated moat is true, what breaks it?
- What stops an incumbent from absorbing the feature?

## Distribution

- Where do the first 10, 100, and 1,000 customers, users, suppliers, or liquidity providers come from?
- Which channel is already working, and what is the evidence?
- Is distribution dependent on one partner, platform, influencer, vendor, or ecosystem?

## Traction And Retention

- Which metrics reflect actual behavior rather than interest?
- What percentage of users or customers repeat, and over what period?
- What does cohort retention show?
- Are customers paying, expanding, referring, or repeatedly returning?

## Economics

- What is the net take after revenue share, payment costs, custody, shipping, incentives, infrastructure, and other pass-throughs?
- What transaction value or volume gets the company to default-alive?
- What holds pricing if powerful participants compress fees or decline to participate?
- What structurally prevents leakage or off-platform transactions?

## Marketplaces And Networks

- Who creates the first supply or liquidity?
- What is the minimum viable density for the network to feel useful?
- How much does reaching that density cost?
- What is the wedge that works before the flywheel works?
- What stops participants from multi-homing or leaving after the company routes demand to a rival?

## Crypto And On-Chain

- Is the chain load-bearing or decorative?
- Is there a wallet in the user flow, or is the experience walletless?
- Are funds or assets held on-chain?
- Is fractionalization on the roadmap?
- What legal review has been done on custody, money transmission, sanctions, consumer protection, and securities exposure?

## Physical Collectibles

- Who authenticates, stores, insures, ships, and remedies the physical asset?
- What happens when item condition, grade, authenticity, or buyer expectation breaks a match?
- Who bears cost when a transaction fails?
- How long is the trust curve versus incumbents?

## The Round

- What is the raise for in concrete milestones?
- What uncertainty does the round remove?
- What evidence will the company need for the next financing?
- What outcome is required at this entry price after dilution?
