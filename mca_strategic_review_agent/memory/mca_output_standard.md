# MCA Output Standard

The external deliverable should be concise, useful in a founder conversation, and strong enough for MCA to place its name on it.

## Required Header

Use:

- MCA Market Intelligence
- Deal Review
- Considerations and Next Steps
- Confidential

## External Structure

1. Opening Note
2. In Brief
3. Our Read
4. SWOT
5. Questions for Next Steps

## Writing Standard

Use strategic opinions, not deck summaries. Each `Our Read` point should include a headline, explanation, and why it matters.

Avoid:

- Generic SWOT points.
- Repeating the deck.
- Empty praise.
- Corporate filler.
- Unsupported certainty.
- Aggressive investor language.
- Claiming external validation when only company-provided evidence exists.

## Density

The client-facing review should be much shorter than the internal analysis. Prefer 3 to 5 strategic conclusions and 6 to 10 next-step questions. Mark the first 2 or 3 questions as priority.
