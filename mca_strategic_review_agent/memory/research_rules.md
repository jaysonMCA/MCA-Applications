# Research Rules

External research is required for a complete review when tools are available. The reviewer should not perform the research manually.

## Research Plan First

Before deep research, create a targeted plan that explains:

- Research area.
- Why it matters.
- What would change MCA's view.
- Source types to check.
- Priority.

Show the plan to the reviewer for approval, additions, or removals.

## Source Quality

Prefer:

- Company primary sources for company claims.
- Official filings, regulatory material, protocol docs, repositories, and product docs.
- Credible market data, investor reports, trade publications, and reputable news.
- Competitor websites, docs, pricing pages, and public announcements.

Avoid weak sourcing for major conclusions. If weak sources are all that exist, label confidence accordingly.

## Freshness

Record research date and publication date where available. Revalidate old sources when the market, regulation, pricing, funding, or product landscape could have changed.

## Evidence Handling

Do not include external research because it is interesting. Include it only when it materially changes the assessment, supports a major conclusion, reveals an opportunity or threat, identifies a contradiction, or creates a diligence question.

## Research Unavailable

If research tools are unavailable, state what cannot be validated and mark opportunities, threats, competitor analysis, and macro claims as preliminary.
